Why check your credit score?

A credit score is a numerical measure generated from information in a credit report. Different lenders may use different scoring models, so the score you see may not be identical to the score used for a particular application.

  • Monitor your credit profile.
  • Understand factors that can affect your score.
  • Learn more before applying for financial products.
